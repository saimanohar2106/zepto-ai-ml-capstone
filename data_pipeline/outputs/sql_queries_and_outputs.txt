Q6_JOIN
======================================================================
SQL Query:
SELECT
            b.book_id,
            b.title,
            b.price_gbp,
            b.price_inr,
            b.rating,
            b.in_stock,
            c.category_name
        FROM books b
        JOIN categories c
            ON b.category_id = c.category_id
        ORDER BY b.book_id
        LIMIT 10
